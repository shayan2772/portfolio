Quick answers

The five questions people ask before booking.

How fast can we start? +
Usually within a week of the intro call. I keep slots intentionally narrow — one engagement at a time — so when I say yes, you're not waiting in a queue.
What does an engagement typically look like? +
Most work falls into 3–10 week sprints with a fixed scope and weekly deliverables, or longer hourly engagements where the scope is genuinely fluid. I'll recommend the shape that fits the work, not the one that pays me more.
Do you sign NDAs / work under confidentiality? +
Yes — NDA-ready. Standard mutual NDA before code or sensitive data. Several current engagements are under confidentiality and not in the public case studies.
Can you take over an existing codebase? +
Often, yes. The first 3–5 days of the engagement are a code + infra audit, with a written assessment of what's salvageable and what needs to be rebuilt. You get the audit even if we don't continue.
What if the honest answer is "you don't need me"? +
Then I tell you on the call. Worst case for you: you leave with a sharper plan and a few specific recommendations — at no cost. I'd rather pass than take work that won't ship well.
Do you do retainers / fractional CTO work? +
Yes, for teams that have shipped something with me already. I don't take retainers cold — we need a delivered project under the belt so the time is spent on the right problems.
